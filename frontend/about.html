About NIVORA

An Intelligent Platform for Accommodation Discovery and Shared Living

NIVORA is a comprehensive platform designed to make accommodation discovery easier, more transparent, and more reliable for students, working professionals, and property owners.

Our Mission

To provide a transparent and efficient platform that connects accommodation seekers with property owners, offering complete visibility into costs and helping people make informed decisions.

Key Features

  • Smart Expense Calculator - Know your exact monthly costs upfront
  • Property Matching - Get recommendations based on your requirements
  • Direct Communication - Connect directly with property owners
  • Transparent Listings - All costs disclosed clearly

For Students & Professionals

Search and filter properties based on your preferences, calculate total living costs, save favorites, and connect directly with property owners.

For Property Owners

List your properties for free, manage inquiries, update availability, and reach thousands of potential tenants.
